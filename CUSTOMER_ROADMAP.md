# CivicLens Customer Roadmap

## Now: reliable civic intake

- Report complaints from text, voice, and evidence photos.
- Classify category, priority, department, confidence, and SLA locally.
- Show every complaint on an OpenStreetMap view with transparent risk signals.
- Let citizens track status, co-sign important reports, and see resolution proof.
- Give administrators one local workspace for dispatch, verification, ward coverage, and calculated metrics.

## Next: stronger operations

- Add offline-first mobile queue synchronization.
- Add exact GPS/geocoding capture with citizen consent.
- Add service-level notifications through email or configurable messaging adapters.
- Add audit exports for ward teams and public transparency reports.
- Add accessibility review, multilingual intake, and low-bandwidth mode.

## Later: evidence-led city planning

- Forecast demand by category and location.
- Compare intervention outcomes against SLA and risk trends.
- Add privacy-preserving aggregation for public dashboards.
- Add model monitoring, drift checks, and human review feedback loops.

## Product boundaries

CivicLens focuses on complaints, service delivery, evidence, and accountable
resolution. It does not profile political representatives or rank people.
